Free and open source · Runs on your Mac

One town. One Pokémon per project.

Working with several coding agents can get confusing. You have to switch between terminals just to see what's happening.

Pokémon Agents gives each project a Pokémon and a place in the town. You can look around to see which agents are working, which have finished, and which are waiting for your reply.

It's a different way to keep track of your coding sessions.

See when an agent needs your help

When an agent asks a question or waits for your approval, its Pokémon comes to find you. You'll see an exclamation mark above its head, just like in the Pokémon games.

You can check what's happening without opening every terminal.

Your Pokémon grows as you code

Your Pokémon gains experience as you work on your projects. Commits, test results, and other coding activity contribute to its progress.

As it grows, it can level up, earn badges, and evolve.

Your projects become a little town

The town gives your coding sessions a place to live. Different locations represent different kinds of work.

Walk around, find your Pokémon, and check what your agents are doing. You can see the state of your projects without reading through a long list of logs.

Work with your agents from the game

When an agent needs an answer, you can respond from the game. You can also open its terminal to see more details.

Start new tasks and check your recent activity without leaving the town every time.

Your work helps your Pokémon grow

As you work on your projects, your Pokémon makes progress too.

  • Gain experience as your coding sessions progress.
  • Earn badges for completing different milestones.
  • Evolve your Pokémon as it meets the requirements.
  • Find shiny Pokémon and see which Pokémon your projects get.

Your Pokémon's progress is connected to your coding activity.

Eight gym badges, earned from real work

You don't grind these. Each badge unlocks on something your coding sessions actually did — your first commit, your first merge, a seven-day streak. Earn the right four and a legendary turns up to patrol your town.

Get your Pokémon town running

Pokémon Agents is free and open source, and runs locally on your Mac. You'll need macOS, Node.js 22, and Claude Code.

1 Install the dependencies
npm install
2 Set up the project
npm run setup
3 Start Pokémon Agents
npm start

Open http://127.0.0.1:7880 in your browser to see your town. View the full setup guide on GitHub →

Runs on your Mac

Pokémon Agents runs on your own machine and opens in your browser. It reads your Claude sessions to show what your agents are doing.

Your session data and game progress are stored locally. The app does not send telemetry.
